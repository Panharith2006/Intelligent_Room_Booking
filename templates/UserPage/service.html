Lecturer Room Booking Policy

1. Booking Time Policy

  • Booking can be submitted at any time (24/7).
  • Booking window: at least 1 hour before start and not more than 1 month ahead.
  • Session duration must be between 1 and 3 hours.
  • Booking in the past is not allowed.

2. Booking Limit Policy

  • Each lecturer may hold up to 5 active bookings at one time.
  • A new booking is allowed when an earlier active booking is completed or cancelled.
  • Consecutive bookings in the same room cannot exceed the maximum per-session duration rule.
  • A lecturer cannot book multiple rooms in the same time slot.

3. Cancellation and Warning Policy

  • Standard cancellation should be made at least 3 hours before start time.
  • Late cancellation can still be processed but is recorded as a penalty.
  • Every 2 late cancellations triggers an admin warning record.
  • Admin may cancel bookings for room unavailability or emergency reasons.

4. Room Usage and Availability Policy

  • Room schedule must clearly show available, occupied, and unavailable periods.
  • Room state can be controlled by admin (including unavailable status).
  • Room capacity limits must be respected for every booking.
  • A short turnover buffer between bookings is enforced; lecturers should leave at least 5 minutes before end time.

5. Conflict and Validation Policy

  • Double booking is automatically blocked.
  • Overlapping time slots are not allowed.
  • Availability is validated before confirmation.

6. Security, AI Usage, and Data Privacy

  • Lecturers are responsible for damages during booking time.
  • Booking logs and activity records are kept for transparency and accountability.
  • Misuse may lead to account suspension.
  • AI assistant suggestions are advisory only; final confirmation depends on system validation.
  • Personal data is stored securely in university systems and not shared with unauthorized third parties.

7. Terms and Lecturer Profile Requirements

  • Rooms must be used for academic or university-related purposes only.
  • No damage to equipment or property is allowed.
  • Cleanliness must be maintained; furniture should be returned to original arrangement.
  • If AC is used, it must be turned off before leaving.
  • Before booking, lecturer profile must be complete and approved by admin.
  • Required verification fields: Lecturer ID, Department, Faculty, Phone number, and Photo.
  • Google OAuth login is supported; only approved lecturer accounts can book rooms.
